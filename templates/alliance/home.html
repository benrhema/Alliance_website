Our Story

From a Classroom
to a Company.

It started in Grade 10 — ten students united by curiosity and a shared obsession with building things. What began as after-school coding sessions grew into something none of us fully planned: a team that ships real software, wins national competitions, and is now formalizing as a registered tech company.

In our senior year we expanded to 12, bringing in two members dedicated to marketing and strategy. Every product we've built has been deployed in a real environment, used by real people. That's not a coincidence — it's our standard.
